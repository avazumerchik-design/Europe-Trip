I # Postcards from Europe

A city-by-city travel notebook for the Feb 2–Mar 31 Europe itinerary. The route starts with an overnight flight to London, continues through Paris, Amsterdam, Prague, Italy, Switzerland, and Spain, and ends back in Madrid. Oxford and Bath are London day-trip options. The home page has a vintage postcard cover and dated route cards; each city page has a day-by-day outline, neighborhood ideas, restaurant and market suggestions, a personal journal, and a postcard gallery. Dates and fares are planning notes, not bookings; confirm the year, schedules, prices, opening times, and availability before travel.

## Editing the site

- Choose **Edit site text** in the header to click and change visible text across the home page and city pages. Changes save automatically in this browser; choose **Save and finish** when you are done.
- Open a city, choose **Edit guide**, and add or update its itinerary, sights, food, nightlife, places to stay, shops, history, local notes, films, books, or current notes. Put one item on each line, then save.
- Add personal entries in **Little things to remember**.
- Upload photos and videos in **Postcards**. Images can be downloaded as postcard-style PNGs.
- Text edits, city guide edits, and journal entries are saved in this browser's local storage. Photos and videos are saved in this browser's IndexedDB. They are not synced to other devices or visitors and can be removed if browser data is cleared.

## Project files

- `index.html` loads the page and the individual city guide files.
- `style.css` contains the responsive styling and city color themes.
- `app.js` renders the home and city pages, and handles text and guide editing, journals, and local media.
- City `.js` files beside `index.html` hold the original guide content. Each registers its content with `addCity()`.
